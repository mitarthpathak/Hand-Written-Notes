	if we train AI that on 33% humidity in  jaipur it was raining and on +14% humidity in pune caused heavy rain then when asked the model can predict that on what % of humidity in ahemdabad it is going to rain...


Deep Learning: 
	subset of machine learning
	this uses multi-layer neural network
	requires a very huge amount of data 
	used for training unstructured data(images, video and audio)

one question: how does AI read the picture?
the picture or any image is converted into the data or the set of data of RGB for each pixel of that picture 
(uses convolutional neural network)

BRANCHES OF THE MACHINE LEARNING:

1. supervised learning: 
	it is majourly learning from labeled examples and there are often correct answers given 
ex - categorising an object as cat or dog

2. unsupervised learning:
	there are no labelled examples here and the model is trained to find the patterns in unlabelled data and predict the structure 
	it can work to uncover the hidden patterns too
	no true info about data is known
ex - calling a kid to categorising the dogs and cat where the kid has no idea what is a dog and what is a cat.

3. Reinforcement learning:
	here the model is basically trained like a pet(dogs and cats and bla bla) think of it like a chess, there are given rewards for the winning moves...

NEURAL NETWORKS:
	in human brain inspired model consisting of nuerons(nodes) arranged in a layer
single neuron: 
	z = g(Σ wᵢxᵢ + b)
x - inputs(features)
w - weights(priority of the inputs)
b - bias(to adjust the threshold)
g - activation function

w and b keeps updating during the training of the models and these are the actual parameters of the model


ANN (artificial neural networks):
	every neuron is connected to the neuron of another layer
	good for tabular data(humidity -> rain)


CNN (convolutional Neural network):
	advanced version of ANN
	special network for images and videos 
	non-tabular data
